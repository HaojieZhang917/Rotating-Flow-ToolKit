# Compressible BEK base-flow primitive residual Gate

Date: 2026-09-12

## Registered scope before calculation

This study substitutes the current `(U,V,W,T,rho)` base state into a unified
steady axisymmetric compressible BEK boundary-layer system written in the
physical normal coordinate `eta`, before the Dorodnitsyn-Howarth transform.
It is an equation-residual Gate for the local similarity base, not a residual
of the full finite-radius Navier-Stokes equations. Radial diffusion and the
higher-order finite-`R` terms discarded in the boundary-layer derivation are
therefore outside its scope.

## Source equations and conventions

Turkyilmazoglu and Uygun (2006), equations (1)-(9), give the compressible
rotating-disk conservation equations, Chapman law `mu=T`, the transform

`y = integral rho d eta`,

and `T=1-(gamma-1) Mr^2 f/2 +(Tw-1)q`. William's thesis chapter 2 gives the
same chain more explicitly: primitive boundary-layer equations (2.19)-(2.24),
transform (2.26), similarity velocities (2.27)-(2.29), transformed equations
(2.30)-(2.32), and thermal equations (2.34)-(2.38).

The general-BEK extension uses Lingwood direct variables and

`Co=2-Ro-Ro^2`, `H_eta=T W`, `rho=1/T`, `mu=T`, `k=T/Pr`.

Here `(U,V,W)` and `H_eta` are velocity profiles scaled by the **signed**
differential rotation `DeltaOmega`. Thus the same profiles expressed using
the positive system-rotation scale carry factors `(Ro U, Ro V, Ro H_eta)`
(with the rigid-frame azimuthal contribution added separately). `H_eta` is
represented in the current code by `H=T*W`; it is not a derivative. This
distinction explains why continuity has no `Ro`, while inertia relative to
viscous diffusion does.

## Primitive-coordinate residuals

With `d_eta = rho d_y` and `r T_r=-2a f`,
`a=(gamma-1)Mr^2/2`, the registered residuals are

```text
Rc = 2 rho U + d_eta(rho H)

Rr = rho[Ro(U^2 + H d_eta U - (V^2-1)) - Co(V-1)]
     - d_eta(mu d_eta U)

Ra = rho[Ro(2UV + H d_eta V) + Co U]
     - d_eta(mu d_eta V)

Rs = rho T - 1

Re = rho Ro[H d_eta T + U(r T_r)]
     - d_eta(k d_eta T)
     - (gamma-1)Mr^2 mu[(d_eta U)^2+(d_eta V)^2].
```

Multiplication by the nonzero factors generated by `d_eta=rho d_y` reduces
the first three residuals to the standard BEK similarity equations. The
energy residual reduces to

```text
T'' - Pr Ro W T' - Pr Ro U(r T_r)
  + (gamma-1)Pr Mr^2[(U')^2+(V')^2] = 0.
```

Equivalently, the literature-compatible thermal component equations in direct
variables are

```text
f'' - Pr Ro W f' - 2Pr Ro U f
    - 2Pr[(U')^2+(V')^2] = 0,
q'' - Pr Ro W q' = 0.
```

At `Ro=-1`, use `W=2 psi` and `U=-psi'`; these expressions recover thesis
equations (2.35)-(2.36) exactly. This endpoint identity fixes the signs without
using the stability operator.

For diagnosis the study also evaluates the thermal residuals of the equations
currently solved in `source_profiles`. Passing only those self-residuals is not
accepted as a physical Gate.

## Numerical study

- flows: `Ro=(-1,0,1)` with the corresponding `Co`;
- thermal cases: `(Mr,Tw)=(0.3,1.0)` for viscous heating and `(0,0.8)` for
  pure wall heat transfer;
- `gamma=1.4`, `Pr=0.72`;
- finite transformed domain `0 <= y <= 40`;
- Chebyshev degrees `N=(80,120,160,200)`;
- residual norms evaluated both on interior nodes with `y<=12` (core) and
  `y<=39` (extended). The second region detects a spurious thermal transition
  displaced towards the artificial endpoint `y=40`;
- every equation reports an absolute infinity norm and
  `absolute_linf/max(term_scale_linf,1)`. The unit floor prevents a
  roundoff-size, one-term equation from being mislabeled as a unit relative
  residual. The raw term-balance ratio is retained separately as
  `term_relative_linf`;
- the complete pointwise primitive residual profiles are retained for `N=120`.

This two-region and normalization refinement was registered after the first
diagnostic run exposed both failure modes and before the preserved `v2`
calculation was executed. The first output directory remains untouched.

The current base-flow backend and all baseline data remain unchanged. Results
are written to a new directory under `work/results/`.

## Results

The authoritative calculation is the preserved `v2` output.  The following
table uses `N=120`, `(Mr,Tw)=(0.3,1)`, and the core region. `velocity max`
is the largest absolute residual among continuity and the two momentum
equations. `energy ratio` is the absolute energy residual divided by the
largest absolute energy-equation term.

| flow | velocity max | state | primitive energy | energy ratio | current-equation self residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| von Karman (`Ro=-1`) | `9.65e-8` | `0` | `4.29e-3` | `9.41e-2` | `8.05e-8` |
| Ekman (`Ro=0`) | `1.47e-7` | `0` | `3.68e-7` | `2.59e-6` | `2.65e-7` |
| Bodewadt (`Ro=1`) | `5.38e-8` | `0` | `1.50e-2` | `1.41e-1` | `4.16e-8` |

The velocity residuals remain below about `3.1e-6` over all four grids; their
increase at `N=200` is consistent with differentiating the interpolated
source solution rather than convergence to a finite residual. In contrast,
the von Karman and Bodewadt primitive energy residuals remain respectively
near `4.3e-3` and `1.5e-2` as `N` increases. They are therefore equation
errors, not spectral truncation errors. The small `current-equation` values
only confirm that the profiles solve the equations used to generate them.

The independent wall-heat case exposes the far-field failure. For von Karman,
the current profile has `q(30)=0.9983`, `q(35)=0.9586`, and `q(39)=0.4710`:
the required transition has been displaced to the artificial boundary at
`y=40`, and the extended primitive energy residual is `1.21e-1`. For
Bodewadt, the transition occurs near the wall but the core residual is
`1.53e-1`. At `Ro=0`, the equation is exactly `q''=0`; the finite-interval
solution is `q=1-y/40`, which satisfies the local differential equation but
has no decaying half-line limit with `q(0)=1` and `q(infinity)=0`.

## Gate decision

**FAIL for the complete compressible base state.** The current velocity base
`(U,V,W)` and algebraic state relation `rho=1/T` pass within the interpolation
and differentiation error of this calculation. The current temperature, and
hence the coupled `(T,rho)` base state, does not satisfy the unified general-
`Ro` primitive energy equation at `Ro=-1` or `Ro=1`; the `Ro=0` thermal
Dirichlet problem is singular on the half-line. Consequently the existing
compressible neutral parameters must remain diagnostic and cannot be promoted
to a self-consistent compressible BEK result until the thermal BVP is replaced
and the residual Gate is rerun.

This is a local similarity boundary-layer Gate. It is not a full finite-radius
Navier--Stokes residual and not a three-dimensional stability validation.
